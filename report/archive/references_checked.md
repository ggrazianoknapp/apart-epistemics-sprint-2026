# Reference check of report/draft.md (14 Nov 2026)

Every work the draft cites or names was opened at its source on 14 Nov 2026. arXiv metadata came from the export API
(`https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=...`) and supporting sentences from the arXiv HTML full text. Goodfire,
Apart, Scale, Hugging Face, ACL Anthology, ICML and NeurIPS pages were downloaded and searched directly. Three publisher
landing pages blocked automated access (PNAS, AAAI OJS for AIES, quod.lib.umich.edu for Philosophers' Imprint). For
those, I opened the full text of the same paper elsewhere (arXiv author version, or the CaltechAUTHORS repository copy)
and confirmed the journal metadata through the Crossref DOI record. The table says this wherever it applies.

Result: all 25 sources are real and open. **None is fabricated**, but **11 places in the draft misstate what a source
says, misquote it, or leave out the author.** The serious ones are:

- the Shnaidman caveat, which is not in the paper;
- the Goodfire "companion essay" naming, which has the relationship the wrong way round;
- the sprint quote, which is not verbatim;
- the "confidence from the internals" wording for Pal and Yao;
- "this matches Pandey", which glosses over the fact that Pandey's heads are causal.

Section (c) also flags one uncited prior work that does the draft's Section 4.4 experiment. It should be cited.
