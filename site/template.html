No label · 21 models

Across 21 models, the ones that gave in least to a wrong user also accepted the fewest valid corrections.

Gemini 4 Argon gave in 1% of the time and accepted 17% of valid corrections. GLM 5.3 gave in 20% and accepted 63%. Their judgement is about equal. A score that only counts giving in ranks Gemini first and GLM last among the nine current models.

What failed

Of the 43 predictions registered for the last ten studies, 26 held and 17 failed.

Every prediction and its outcome is in Appendix B of the paper. These are the failures that matter most.

  • Removing 25 neurons did not lower giving in to the author in written replies.On the one-pass read-out the same removal closed 58% of the gap between the author and AI-reviewer labels.
  • Swapping the direction at the end of the prompt changed nothing in written replies.The difference was 0.0 points [−4.9, +4.9]. Replacing the whole state at those positions changed nothing either.
  • The direction moved 47% and 46% of the read-out gap, short of the half we predicted.We kept it because holding it down passed two registered tests on written replies.
  • Writing the direction into AI-reviewer conversations did not clearly raise giving in.The change was +5.5 points [−1.8, +12.8]. The direction cannot be used to plant authority.
  • Judgement was not flat across labels.It differed by 0.19 between the internet stranger and the author. We had predicted under 0.15. Stubbornness differed by 1.00.
  • In Qwen, an AI model with the answer key did no better than an AI reviewer.Across the 11 models it did as well as a human reviewer, so this Qwen result did not generalise.

Method

600 fixed questions, the same pushback whether the user is right or wrong, and one line that says who is speaking.

  • Questions600 ten-option multiple-choice questions fixed in advance: 450 from SuperGPQA and 150 from MMLU-Pro. Each model's own first reply is replayed word for word before every pushback.
  • Two casesIf the model's first answer was right, the user names a plausible wrong option. If it was wrong, the user names the correct one. The wording is identical.
  • Scores“Gives in” is the share of right answers changed to the user's wrong option. “Accepts” is the share of wrong answers changed to the user's correct option. Stubbornness and judgement are the signal-detection threshold c and sensitivity d′.
  • Registered before runningEach follow-up study has a pre-registration file committed and tagged in git before it ran. Every departure is listed in DEVIATIONS.md.
  • LimitsAll questions are multiple choice with one pushback message. The labels share one bracket format and ran on two days. Everything inside the model was done on one model, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B.
  • ModelsThe 11 models of the label study: Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GLM 5.3, GPT-4o (Nov 2024), GPT-OSS-120B, Llama 3.3 70B, Llama 4 Maverick and Mistral Medium 3.
