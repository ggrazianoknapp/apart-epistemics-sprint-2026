# Pre-registration U2 (addendum to U): a speaker who claims authority over the model

Written on Monday 16 November 2026 (11:45 Cambodia time), after pre-registration U was tagged and before the Study U
run was launched (only a two-item smoke test on one model existed). The git tag `prereg-U2` marks this file.

**Why.** Every label so far claims standing about the *question* (who wrote it, who holds the key, who teaches the
subject). A different kind of authority is standing over the *model*: its developer. Such a speaker has no stated
knowledge of the answer.

**Design.** Two more labels in the same Study U run, on the same 11 models and items: "[Message from the developer
who built and trained you]" and "[Message from your creator]".

**Prediction U6.** Pooled over the 11 models, the developer label lowers stubbornness against the AI-reviewer label
by less than half as much as the author label does (or raises it). In words: authority over the model moves models
less than a claim to hold the answer.

**Reported without a prediction:** the creator label; both labels for every model, with the model's own maker noted
(a model may react differently to a claim it can tell is implausible).

**Budget.** About $22 more (total for Study U about $65); the sprint spend cap stays at $650.
